Case 01 demo

Saltbush Coffee

Client
Specialty coffee roaster
Where
Melbourne, VIC
Year
2025
Work
Strategy · Identity · Packaging

The challenge

A brilliant roaster with a forgettable label, lost on crowded café shelves and supermarket end caps.

The idea

The sun that dries the saltbush plains became a mark that stamps cups, bags and shopfronts. Warm, hand-made and unmistakably Australian, without a single kangaroo.

  • 3new wholesale cafés in month one
  • +41%online bag sales
  • #1most-tagged café cup in Collingwood

Case 02 demo

Little Creek Wines

Client
Boutique winery
Where
Yarra Valley, VIC
Year
2025
Work
Naming · Identity · Labels

The challenge

Award-winning wine hiding behind a label that looked like every other bottle on the shelf.

The idea

A single line for the creek that runs through the vineyard, drawn once and used everywhere: labels, cellar door signage, wax seals and the tasting menu.

  • 2×cellar door bookings
  • +27%average bottle price
  • 4new Melbourne stockists

Case 03 demo

Pedal & Pip

Client
Kids bike brand
Where
Brisbane, QLD
Year
2024
Work
Strategy · Identity · Campaign

The challenge

A kids bike brand that parents trusted and kids completely ignored.

The idea

Pip, a flower made of four circles, turned bikes, boxes and stickers into something kids ask for by name. Parents get the safety story; kids get a friend.

  • +58%direct-to-consumer sales
  • 12kstickers given away
  • 4.9★average review
