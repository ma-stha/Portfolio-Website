  1. Melbourne eCommerce Brand

    The problem

    Ad spend was spread across 30+ campaigns with no consistent conversion tracking. ROAS had fallen to 1.4x and the team could not tell which channel was driving revenue.

    What I did

    Rebuilt GA4 and server-side tagging first, then consolidated campaigns into Performance Max, Search and Retargeting. Introduced weekly creative testing and a budget model based on marginal ROAS.

    The result

    ROAS climbed from 1.4x to 3.6x in four months while total spend stayed flat. Revenue attributed to paid media grew 118%.

  2. Multi-location Service Business

    The problem

    The business relied on word of mouth and paid leads. Organic search contributed under 5% of enquiries and the site had no location pages.

    What I did

    Built a suburb-level location page architecture, fixed Core Web Vitals, ran a Google Business Profile routine and a monthly content cadence for problem-aware searches.

    The result

    Organic enquiries grew 212% year on year and became the largest lead source. Blended cost per lead dropped by a third.

  3. B2B SaaS Startup

    The problem

    Strong traffic growth but a 1.1% visitor-to-trial rate. Marketing and product disagreed on what to fix first.

    What I did

    Instrumented the funnel in GA4, ran session recordings and interviews, then prioritised nine experiments. Shipped a new pricing page, a shorter signup form and intent-based CTAs.

    The result

    Trial conversion rose to 2.7% with trial-to-paid unchanged, which meant 145% more new MRR from the same traffic.

    Trial conversion
    +2.7%
    New MRR
    +145%
    Experiments

  4. DTC Wellness Brand

    The problem

    Email was one weekly newsletter sent to everyone. Repeat purchase rate was 18% and there were no automated flows.

    What I did

    Designed seven automated flows, segmented the list by purchase behaviour and ran a monthly campaign calendar with subject-line and offer testing.

    The result

    Email went from 6% to 31% of revenue. Repeat purchase rate rose to 34% and the flows alone out-earned the total marketing spend.

Approach

A loop, not a launch.

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    Audit the data, the funnel and the market. Find where revenue leaks before touching a single ad.

  2. 02

    Strategy

    Set measurable targets, pick the channels that can hit them, and agree how success is judged.

  3. 03

    Build

    Tracking, campaigns, pages and flows. Everything instrumented from day one so nothing runs blind.

  4. 04

    Measure

    Weekly reporting against the targets, in dashboards you can open without me.

  5. 05

    Scale

    Double down on what the data proves, cut what it doesn't, and compound the wins.
