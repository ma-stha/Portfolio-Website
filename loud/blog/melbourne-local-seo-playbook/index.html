A suburb-by-suburb playbook for ranking in Melbourne

Melbourne customers search by suburb. Here is how to build pages, profiles and proof that rank in the suburbs you actually serve, without creating thin doorway pages.

      TL;DR

      • People search "plumber Fitzroy", not "plumber Melbourne". Build for the suburbs you really serve.
      • One genuinely useful page per suburb beats fifty copy-pasted ones. Google's spam policies call out doorway pages.
      • Your Google Business Profile and reviews do as much work as your website. Measure leads by suburb.

      Melbourne is a city of suburbs, and its search behaviour follows. Local searchers add a suburb, a postcode or "near me", and Google answers with the map pack plus a handful of local pages. Here's the playbook I use for service businesses.

      01Build a suburb architecture

      Start with your services, then add a page for each suburb you genuinely serve and want more work in. Link them in a clear tree so both people and Google understand the structure.

      !

      Avoid doorway pages. Pages that swap only the suburb name are exactly what Google's spam policies warn about. Each page needs something real and local.

      What makes a suburb page real: photos of jobs done there, the problems common to that area's housing or businesses, travel time and availability, local reviews and a short FAQ. if you can't write 5 true sentences about a suburb, don't make the page.

      02Make your Google Business Profile do the heavy lifting

      • Choose the most specific primary category, then add relevant secondary ones.
      • Set accurate service areas, opening hours and services with descriptions.
      • Upload real photos regularly, especially of completed work.
      • Use posts for offers, seasonal reminders and new projects.

      03Earn reviews and local proof

      Ask every happy customer, every time, with a direct link. Reply to every review. When customers naturally mention the suburb or the job, that detail helps people choosing between you and a competitor down the road.

      Local links matter too: suburb business associations, sponsorships of local clubs and events, and genuine partnerships with nearby businesses.

      04Write for problem-aware searches

      Many searches happen before someone knows which service they need: "why is my hot water lukewarm", "cost to fix a blocked drain Melbourne". Answer those questions plainly, with prices or ranges where you can, and link to the relevant service and suburb pages.

      05Measure leads by suburb

      Rankings are a means, not the goal. Track enquiries by landing page, and you'll see which suburbs earn their keep.
