01 — About

A marketer who reads the numbers before the headlines.

Most marketing fails quietly: budgets drift, tracking breaks, and nobody notices until the quarter is over. I started my career on the content side, then became the person who kept asking "but what did it actually do?". That question turned into a method.

Today I help Melbourne businesses build marketing they can measure. Clean analytics first, then search and paid campaigns that are judged on revenue, then the experiments that compound. I work as an independent consultant and I'm open to the right in-house role.
